Data format

An experiment is two files: a small description (experiment.json) and a table of plate-reader values (readings.csv). The published experiments in this site are stored in exactly this format.

Minimum required information

  • About the experiment: a short id, a title, the species, a contact, the start date, and the container the animals were incubated in.
  • For every well or cup: a unique sample_id, the plate (or run) it belongs to, and whether it is a sample or a blank.
  • For every reading: the hour since resazurin was added and the raw fluorescence.
  • For every sample: the incubation temperature in °C.
  • A reading at hour 0 for every well, and at least one later reading. Hour 0 is the baseline for fold change.

Strongly recommended: at least one blank well (resazurin solution, no animal) per plate, and shell length in mm. Without blanks the site cannot blank-correct; without lengths it cannot size-normalize.

readings.csv

One row per well per time point (long format). Comma- or tab-separated, with a header row. Column order does not matter.

What the site calculates

  

    
ValueDefinition

    
Fold changefluorescence at hour t ÷ fluorescence of the same well at hour 0

    
Blank-corrected fold changefold change − mean fold change of the blank wells on the same plate at the same hour

    
Per mmblank-corrected fold change ÷ shell length in mm (the paper's "metabolism")

    
Rateblank-corrected fold change at the last reading ÷ hours elapsed

  


  
Rules the validator enforces

  
    
    
  • sample_id is unique within the experiment, and each well has one reading per hour.
    • 
    
  • type is sample or blank. Empty shells and other controls are samples with a group label.
    • 
    
  • plate names the set of wells that share blanks. If blanks were run per temperature or per day, give each set its own plate name.
    • 
    
  • Every well has a positive reading at hour 0 and at least two readings. Empty fluorescence cells are skipped.
    • 
    
  • temperature_c is present for every sample. For two-phase designs, record the stress temperature and describe the schedule in temperature_profile.
    • 
    
  • Wells you want kept but hidden by default (spilled, saturated, empty shell) get a reason in excluded.

About

  
This site is a static page served from the docs/ folder of its GitHub repository. It has no server and no database: the published data are bundled as one file, and everything else happens in your browser.

Caveats

  
Fold change depends on plate reader gain, resazurin volume per animal, and the temperature of the solution at the hour-0 read, so absolute values differ between studies for reasons other than biology. Compare shapes and within-study contrasts first, and treat cross-study differences in level with care.
